💡 Quick guide

Credit — money any member adds into the common fund.

Expense — money spent: electricity, gas, maid salary, groceries, etc.

Saving — money parked into an asset: Mutual Fund, RD, FD, Stocks, Gold, Land, Insurance.

Fund Balance = Contributions − Expenses − Savings. Your savings still show up under Savings & Assets as your growing wealth.
